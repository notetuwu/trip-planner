Your trips, on this phone

Open the trip file from the planner (Export → For phone) here. It is kept on this phone and works without a connection: today's stops, the plan, expenses and the journal. Nothing is uploaded.

First, add this page to your Home Screen
  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Trips from the Home Screen and pick your trip file there.

From the Home Screen it works offline and keeps what you enter. In the browser itself, an iPhone may clear it after a week.

Get the file onto the phone however you like (AirDrop, iCloud Drive, a message to yourself), then pick it from Files. A newer file of the same trip replaces the old one; what you entered here stays.
